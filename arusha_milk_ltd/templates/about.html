Serving Since 2012

Our Story

Started on a small farm near Usa River, we now deliver fresh milk across Arusha.

What began with three local farmers has grown into a trusted dairy. We invested in cooling tanks, hygienic packaging, and a dedicated delivery team. Today we supply hundreds of homes, schools, and hotels with pure, unadulterated milk – still sourced only from Arusha region.

Our Vision

To be the benchmark for quality and transparency in Tanzania’s dairy industry. We believe fresh milk should taste like milk – not powder, not chemicals. We’re building a direct‑from‑farm model that rewards farmers and nourishes our community.
