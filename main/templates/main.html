About Green Land Company Ltd

Delivering Fresh, Quality Agricultural & Dairy Products Across Tanzania

Green Land Company Ltd is committed to providing high-quality agricultural and dairy products that meet the highest standards of freshness, safety, and nutrition. We proudly serve households, retailers, and businesses with products sourced and processed with care and professionalism.

  • Our Mission
  • Our Vision
  • Our Goal

Our mission is to produce and distribute safe, nutritious, and affordable agricultural and dairy products while supporting local farmers and contributing to sustainable farming practices in Tanzania.

Our vision is to become a leading and trusted agricultural and dairy company in East Africa, recognized for quality, innovation, and excellence in customer satisfaction.

Our goal is to continuously improve product quality, expand distribution networks, empower farming communities, and ensure that every customer receives fresh and reliable products.
